\# Q1 — Scrum Team Roles



\## Project

College ERP System



\## Objective

To define the Scrum roles and responsibilities for planning and developing the College ERP student features using Jira.



\## 1. Product Owner



\*\*Role:\*\* Student Representative (simulated stakeholder)



\*\*Responsibilities:\*\*

\- Represent student requirements and expectations.

\- Identify and clarify required College ERP features.

\- Prioritize the Product Backlog according to business value.

\- Review completed functionality against acceptance criteria.

\- Provide feedback during the Sprint Review.



\## 2. Scrum Master



\*\*Role:\*\* Student Project Coordinator



\*\*Responsibilities:\*\*

\- Facilitate sprint planning and daily Scrum discussions.

\- Help the team follow Scrum practices.

\- Monitor progress using the Jira Scrum Board.

\- Identify blockers and coordinate their resolution.

\- Support the Sprint Review and Retrospective.



\## 3. Development Team



\*\*Role:\*\* Student Developer and Tester



\*\*Responsibilities:\*\*

\- Analyze the user stories and acceptance criteria.

\- Plan tasks for login, academic dashboard, attendance, and examination results.

\- Implement the planned functionality.

\- Test the completed work against the acceptance criteria.

\- Demonstrate the working increment during the Sprint Review.



\## 4. Role Allocation Note



This assignment uses simulated Scrum responsibilities for an academic demonstration. The role labels represent the responsibilities required by Scrum and do not imply that additional real team members or Jira accounts have been created.



\## 5. Scrum Artifacts



\- \*\*Product Backlog:\*\* Prioritized College ERP user stories.

\- \*\*Sprint Backlog:\*\* Selected stories and tasks planned for the sprint.

\- \*\*Increment:\*\* The set of completed and verified functionality produced during the iteration.



\## Conclusion



The Scrum roles define responsibility for backlog prioritization, facilitation, development, testing, and review. Jira is used to track the planned work and its progress.

\# Q1 — Sprint Backlog



\## 1. Project Details



Project: College ERP System



Sprint Name: College ERP Sprint 1



Sprint Goal:

Provide students with a planned and testable flow for login and access to academic information, including the dashboard, attendance, and examination results.



Sprint Duration: 5 working days



\## 2. Selected User Stories



| Order | User Story | Priority | Estimate |

|---|---|---|---:|

| 1 | Student Login to College ERP | Highest | 5 story points |

| 2 | View Academic Dashboard | High | 3 story points |

| 3 | View Student Attendance | High | 5 story points |

| 4 | View Examination Results | High | 3 story points |



Total planned estimate: 16 story points.



These are planning estimates for the academic simulation, not measured development effort.



\## 3. Sprint Tasks and Assignments



| Task | Responsibility | Planned Timeline |

|---|---|---|

| Review requirements and acceptance criteria | Product Owner — simulated student representative | Day 1 |

| Finalize sprint backlog and resolve requirement questions | Scrum Master — student project coordinator | Day 1 |

| Plan login validation and authentication tests | Development Team — student developer and tester | Day 1–2 |

| Plan academic dashboard and navigation | Development Team — student developer and tester | Day 2–3 |

| Plan attendance display and percentage validation | Development Team — student developer and tester | Day 3–4 |

| Plan examination-results display and access checks | Development Team — student developer and tester | Day 4 |

| Review acceptance criteria and test planned functionality | Development Team — student developer and tester | Day 5 |

| Conduct Sprint Review and Retrospective | Scrum team and simulated stakeholder | Day 5 |



The role assignments represent simulated responsibilities for the academic exercise.



\## 4. Daily Scrum Plan



A short Daily Scrum is planned for each working day.



The team discusses:



1\. What work was completed since the previous meeting?

2\. What work will be undertaken next?

3\. Are there blockers or requirement changes?



Progress is tracked through the Jira Scrum Board.



\## 5. Definition of Done



A story may be marked Done when:



\- Its acceptance criteria have been checked.

\- Relevant functional or validation tests have passed, where implemented.

\- Any identified defects have been addressed or documented.

\- The resulting work has been reviewed.

\- The Jira issue status reflects the actual result.



\## 6. Sprint Review Plan



At the end of the sprint, the team reviews the completed work against the acceptance criteria.



The Product Owner role represents student expectations and provides feedback. Any incomplete work or requested changes are recorded for the Product Backlog.



\## 7. Sprint Retrospective Plan



The team discusses what went well, what could be improved, and what action should be tried in the next sprint.



Example retrospective actions:



\- Well-defined acceptance criteria reduce ambiguity.

\- Small tasks make progress easier to track.

\- Testing should be planned alongside development.

\- Requirement changes should be recorded and reprioritized.



These are proposed retrospective points until the review is actually conducted.



\## 8. Sprint Artifacts



\- Product Backlog: prioritized College ERP user stories.

\- Sprint Backlog: selected stories, tasks, responsibilities, and timelines.

\- Increment: completed and verified work demonstrated at Sprint Review.



The actual completed increment and sprint outcome must be recorded after the relevant work has been performed.



\## Conclusion



The sprint backlog organizes the College ERP requirements into a five-day planning exercise. It assigns responsibilities, estimates work, and establishes a schedule for daily tracking, review, and retrospective activities.
